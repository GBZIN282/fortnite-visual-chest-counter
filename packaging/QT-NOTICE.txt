Qt for Python (PySide6) - the desktop app's window toolkit
==========================================================

The window of Fortnite Visual Chest Counter is made with Qt for Python (PySide6 + Shiboken6) {version} and the Qt {version}
libraries, (c) The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors. They are used under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 3 (LGPL-3.0-only); the licence texts are LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt next to this file.

The files are the unmodified ones from the official PyPI wheels PySide6-Essentials {version} and shiboken6 {version}
(signed by The Qt Company Oy). Only the parts this app needs are included: the QtCore, QtGui and QtWidgets modules
and the Windows platform plugin (runtime\lib\PySide6, runtime\lib\shiboken6).
They are separate, dynamically loaded libraries: you may replace them with your own compatible build of the same modules.
